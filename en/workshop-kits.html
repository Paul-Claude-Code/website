How a kit works

Buy it, read it, run it.

Step 1

Buy the kit

Instant download. Slides, Miro board and guide in one folder.

Step 2

Block the time

Copy the invitation template, invite your team, block 2.5 hours.

Step 3

Read the guide

20 minutes before the workshop. It sits next to you while you facilitate, so there's nothing to memorise.

Step 4

Run the workshop

You follow the guide, your team works in the Miro board. Real results.
