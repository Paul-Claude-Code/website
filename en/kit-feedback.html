FAQ

Your questions.
Honest answers.

Do I need facilitation experience?
No. The guide tells you word for word what to say and when, including tips for typical reactions. You don't need to know it by heart, it sits right next to you. If you'd like extra confidence, "Kit + coaching support" includes a 30-minute call beforehand to prepare you for your specific team, and a debrief afterwards about what happened in the room.
Isn't 2.5 hours a long time?
It's not a meeting. It's protected time for something that never happens in the daily grind. No buzzword bingo, just real work. In our experience the time goes by faster than you'd expect.
What if my team has never used Miro?
No problem. The board includes a short introduction. Miro is free and runs in the browser. Your team doesn't need an account, just a link.
Does it work in person, too?
Yes. Remote, hybrid, or in the room with a projector. The guide has specific tips for all three.
When do I get the kit?
Right after you buy it. The kit arrives automatically by email, with all the materials ready to download.
The topic is too heated in our team. Can I still facilitate on my own?
For heated topics we recommend booking facilitation. A LEAP coach comes in as a neutral outsider and you simply take part. You still have the kit as the basis.
Can I use the workshop more than once?
The kit covers one workshop with one team. To repeat it (say, as a refresher) or use it with another team, you'll need a new kit.
Are the materials available in English?
Not yet. The slides, Miro board and guide are currently in German. If you need them in English for your team or organization, let us know.
Can I use the kit with my own clients (e.g. as a trainer or consultant)?
No. The license only covers your own team or organization. If you want to use the kit with your own clients, get in touch. We're planning a separate academy license for that.
