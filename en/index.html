The reality

You're a good leader. But nobody ever taught you how leadership really works.

There are plenty of tools, methods and frameworks out there. What classic seminars ignore is that 80% of our decisions are driven by emotion, not logic.

That's why slides alone don't change much. At LEAP you spend less time in seminar mode and more time in real situations, with play, fun and fast progress.

80% of our decisions are driven by emotion. That's why joy and leadership belong together.
