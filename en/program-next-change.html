What makes this program different

You run ready-made workshops with your own team.

Unlike most leadership programs, you put the workshops into practice yourself, with your team. So you don't just build your own skills as a leader, you see and feel the effect on your team right away.

Leaders don't have much time. This program gives you tools and formats you don't just learn about in a seminar room, but try out yourself with your team.

Programmaufbau

Deine Reise in 5 Stationen.

6 Monate, praxisnah, in Co-Creation mit dir und deinen Mitreisenden. Die 3 Module sind fertige Workshop-Kits: Vertrauen aufbauen · Rollen & Verantwortung · Feedback — jedes komplett mit Präsentation, Miro-Board und Moderationsleitfaden.

Kick-Off
We get started together
We get to know each other, connect with the other leaders, make a good plan for the months ahead and start preparing for Module 1.
Module 1
Building Trust
You run the workshop kit with your team and build psychological safety. Everyone is nice, but who really speaks openly? This module changes that.
See the workshop kit →
Module 2
Roles & Responsibility
With this kit you clarify who's responsible for what and how you really work together. No more duplicated work, no more open questions.
See the workshop kit →
Module 3
Feedback
With this kit you build a real feedback culture in your team. Not just once a year in the annual review, but regularly, honestly and in a way that helps.
