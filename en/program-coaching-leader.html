Why this program

Leading doesn't always mean having the answer.

Every day you face situations where instructions and advice aren't the right response. Sometimes your team needs someone who asks good questions, not someone who answers them.

Coaching as a Leader gives you a concrete tool for exactly those moments. Not theory, but practice, from the very first session.

As a leader you play many roles. Coach is one of the most effective, and the one people practise least.

Was du lernst

Konkret. Praktisch.
Sofort anwendbar.

Vier inhaltliche Schwerpunkte — immer verknüpft mit direkter Übung und Reflexion.

Focus 01

Your roles as a leader

Leader, adviser, coach: which role works best when? You learn to switch between them and decide deliberately what the situation needs.

Focus 02

Systemic coaching basics

From what coaching is and the mindset behind it to concrete questioning techniques, you get the craft that makes coaching work. As practice you do, not theory you read.

Focus 03

Practising in every role

Coach, coachee, observer: you practise all three and reflect straight afterwards. Including reflecting teams, a structured technique for looking deeper at coaching conversations.

Focus 04

Effective goals & a development plan

You learn to set coaching goals that work, and you leave with a self-directed development plan for making what you've learned part of your everyday leadership.
