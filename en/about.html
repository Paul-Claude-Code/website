About LEAP

We believe joy
and leadership belong together.

LEAP stands for Leading & Empowering Awesome People. We develop leaders and teams by doing, not in the seminar room. With a steep learning curve and a lot of fun failing along the way.

Why LEAP exists

Classic seminars teach knowledge. But leadership happens in the moment.

80% of our decisions are driven by emotion, not logic. Most trainings ignore that, and that's exactly where LEAP comes in.

We always get hands-on, because that's the only place you can fail, learn and grow. Less theory, more real situations, more play, fun and fast progress.

If you want to lead people, you first have to learn to lead yourself, without losing the joy along the way.

— Paul Scheipl · Founder of LEAP
What we stand for

0% bullshit
