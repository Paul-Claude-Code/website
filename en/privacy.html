Privacy policy

How we handle your data, transparently and in line with the GDPR.

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is:
LEAP e.U., Spittelauer Lände 15-2/7/21, 1090 Vienna, Austria
Email: paul@ich-leaps.at · Phone: +43 664 915 31 84

2. General information on data processing

As a rule, we only process our users' personal data to the extent necessary to provide a working website and our content and services. Processing generally only takes place with the user's consent or where permitted by law (Art. 6(1) GDPR).

3. Hosting

This website is hosted by an external service provider. The data collected by the hosting provider (including IP address, time of access and pages visited) is processed to ensure the technical operation of the website. Processing is based on our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and efficiently (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Contacting us

If you contact us via the contact form, email or phone, we store your details to handle your enquiry and any follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering your enquiry). The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed for handling your enquiry.

5. Cookies & analytics tools

This website uses cookies that are technically necessary to run the site. To measure reach we use Umami (Umami Cloud, umami.is), a privacy-friendly analytics tool that uses no cookies and creates no personal profiles. Only aggregated, anonymised usage data is collected (e.g. pages visited, time on page, device type). The legal basis is our legitimate interest in analysing and improving our services (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If marketing tools with cookies are added in future, we will inform you beforehand via a cookie banner and ask for your consent.

6. Leadership Navigator

In the Leadership Navigator (German only) you can use the "Fehlt was?" field to send us a free-text suggestion and, optionally, your email address. This information is sent to us by email and used only to handle your suggestion or to reply to you if you left an email address. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in developing our services) or Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR if you ask us to reply by email.

7. Team Check

When you complete the Team Check on this website, we store your answers (values from 1 to 5), the resulting score, the time and, where applicable, the source you came from (e.g. LinkedIn). We store neither your name nor your email address nor your IP address, so we cannot link the data to you. We use this data only in aggregate to develop the Team Check and to publish general analyses. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving our services (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you use the Team Check together with your team, we store all participants' answers under a random team ID that only appears in the team link. Everyone who has this link can see the team's average for each area. The answers of the person who created the team link are also only included anonymously in this average. Your own result is stored and shown only in your browser. The average is only shown once at least three people from the team have taken part, and individual answers are never shown. So only share the team link with people who should see the result. If you ask for your result by email, we pass your first name, email address and result (result type, score per area and a link to your result) to our email provider Brevo (Sendinblue GmbH, Berlin). After you confirm (double opt-in), Brevo sends you your result and, a week later, a short follow-up. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via the unsubscribe link in every email.

8. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification or erasure
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to data portability

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority about the processing of your personal data. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at).

9. Storage period

Unless a more specific storage period is stated in this privacy policy, your personal data remains with us until the purpose of processing no longer applies. Statutory retention periods remain unaffected.

10. Changes to this privacy policy

We reserve the right to update this privacy policy so that it always meets current legal requirements. The version in force at the time of your visit applies.

11. Usage rights for workshop kits

(1) Upon full payment, the buyer receives a simple, non-transferable right to use the purchased workshop kits to run one workshop with one team. Repeating the workshop or using it with another team requires buying the kit again.

(2) In particular, the following are not covered: passing the kits on to other teams or third parties, reselling them, making them publicly available, and using them to provide paid services to third-party clients (e.g. training, consulting, facilitation). LEAP offers separate academy licenses for commercial use with third-party clients.

(3) In the event of a breach of these terms of use, LEAP reserves the right to claim injunctive relief and damages.
