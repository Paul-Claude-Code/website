Workshop Kit · Feedback Culture in the Team

Thank you! Your kit is here.

Great that you're taking this on with your team. Below you'll find what's arriving in the next few minutes and how best to get started. Preparation takes about 25 minutes, and the guide walks you through the rest.

What happens next

Two emails in your inbox

from no-reply@myablefy.com

Invoice and downloads

It contains the links to the presentation, facilitator guide, agenda and email templates, each in English and German. You can use the same link to download everything again later.

from LEAP

Miro board and all materials

It has the link to your own Miro board, fully set up. Your team won't need a Miro account. All materials are attached, in English and German.

Nothing there yet? Please check your spam folder. If nothing has arrived after fifteen minutes, write to us and we'll send everything to you directly.

How to start

Four steps to your workshop

  1. 1

    Read the guide

    Start with the facilitator guide, not the slides. It lists every step with timings and the exact words you can use. Afterwards you'll know how the workshop feels.

  2. 2

    Set a date and invite your team

    Pick a date when everyone can really be there, not answering emails on the side. For the invitation, use the email templates and adapt them.

  3. 3

    Click through the Miro board once

    Open the board from the second email and take a quick look at each section. Share the link with your team on the day of the workshop.

  4. 4

    Run the workshop

    Slides on the screen, board open, guide next to you. The kit is yours, and you can use it with any team as often as you like.

A tip from our experience

Start with yourself

In the week before the workshop, explicitly ask one person for feedback on something you did. Listen, say thank you and don't explain. Once your team has seen that, people will dare a lot more in the workshop.
